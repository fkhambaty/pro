# Vertical decision — locked for 12 months

**Status:** LOCKED

**Vertical:** Coaching institutes / tuition centres / test-prep (India)  
**Primary metro (brand density):** Pune  
**Secondary (year 2 only):** Mumbai, then Bengaluru

## Why this vertical

- Large, fragmented SMB base; owners are non-technical laymen
- Clear, repeatable workflows: fees, batches, attendance, parent WhatsApp, results
- Packageable into three SKUs without “build anything”
- Education-adjacent shipping instinct already proven (Funberry-class products)
- Vertical SaaS exists but is often rigid or expensive for small centres — room for packaged done-for-you

## Who we sell to (ICP)

| Attribute | Spec |

| Business | Coaching / tuition / test-prep centre |

| Buyer | Owner or admin who lives on WhatsApp |

## Who we do not sell to (year 1)

- Full K-12 schools needing ERP / board compliance
- EdTech startups wanting custom SaaS products
- “Build me the next Byju’s / Unacademy”
- Enterprises and multi-state chains (P2+)

## Kill rule

If after 90 days we cannot close 5 packages in Pune coaching, we revisit SKUs — we do **not** open a general marketplace or switch to “any business worldwide.”
