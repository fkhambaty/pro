## Global exclusions (all SKUs)

These are **never** included. Say no, or quote as a separate product later.

- Native Android / iOS App Store apps
- Custom AI tutors / chatbot teachers
- LMS with video hosting / DRM
- Biometric attendance hardware
- Accounting / GST filing / Tally replacement
- Integrations outside the published list per SKU
- Content writing, photography, or SEO retainers
- Multi-city franchise ERP
- Anything not listed in the Canon screens for that order

**Late rule:** If go-live slips more than 7 calendar days past the committed date for reasons on FORMA, deposit is refundable on request (Canon must still be signed; client delays pause the clock).
