# Partner agreement outline (non-lawyer draft)

Use counsel before signing. This is an operating outline for year-1 channel partners.

## Parties

FORMA Operator (“FORMA”) and Partner (“Partner”).

## Scope

Partner refers coaching / tuition prospects in the Pune metro for FORMA catalog SKUs only.

## Referral definition

A referred lead becomes payable when:

1. Partner is named at lead creation, and
2. Canon is signed within 90 days of intro, and
3. M3 (go-live) payment is received.

## Fees

- 10% of Build X paid within 14 days of M3, **or**
- ₹5,000 client discount in lieu of partner cash (documented on Canon)

Care residual (optional addendum): 5% of collected Care Y for 12 months.

## Conduct

- No misrepresentation of FORMA as freelancers marketplace or “AI builds alone”
- No collecting client software payments into partner accounts
- Confidentiality of pricing and client data

## Term

12 months, renewable. Either party may exit with 30 days notice; fees owed for closed deals remain payable.
